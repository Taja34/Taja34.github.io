Political campaign management platform

Mayoral campaigns in mid-sized Colombian towns run on Excel, WhatsApp groups and notebooks: duplicated lists, no territorial control, and personal data collected without consent. I designed a platform that shows how many real supporters a campaign has, where they are and who brought them in, while complying with Colombia's data protection law.

  • Multi-tenant architecture: isolation between campaigns is enforced with PostgreSQL Row Level Security, not just in the UI.
  • Permissions by role and scope (territory, sector or own records), with an audit log of every change.
  • Encrypted national ID numbers, a versioned privacy policy and data-subject requests tracked against legal deadlines.
  • Mobile-first quick sign-up with duplicate detection, filtered search and saved views.
  • Territorial catalog using DANE's official DIVIPOLA codes.

Algorithmic trading bot

A system that trades crypto on Binance with fixed rules and strict risk management, taking emotion out of the decisions. I validated the strategy with data before risking money and moved it through phases: backtest, testnet and live trading.

  • Custom backtest engine with fees, out-of-sample validation and Monte Carlo simulation of drawdown risk.
  • Tested market filters, more coins and risk levels; only what improved results went to production.
  • Two live strategies in separate accounts, with an automatic kill switch if drawdown exceeds the limit.
  • Telegram alerts, trade logging and automatic start on Windows.

How I work

From problem to shipped product

  1. Understand

    I talk to the people who will use the system and write down scope, roles and decisions before coding.

  2. Design

    Database, permissions and screens in short documents the client can review.

  3. Build in stages

    Every stage ends in something usable, with automated tests and AI-assisted development reviewed line by line.

  4. Measure

    Real data against what was expected, and changes only when the numbers justify them.
